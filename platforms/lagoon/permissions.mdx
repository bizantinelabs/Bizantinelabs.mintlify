## Roles

| Role | Where held | Typical implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curator | Safe \+ Zodiac Role Modifier or MPC | Verifies NAV, settles, manages strategy |
| Valuation provider | Bizantine-run or Lagoon-run — verify | Proposes NAV |
| Whitelist manager | Curator or delegate | Depositor allowlist |
| Vault admin | Owner | Fees, configuration |

## Bizantine role holdings

<Warning>
  No Bizantine Lagoon vault has been provided. Every role holder above is `NOT VERIFIED`. Curator Safe address, threshold, valuation-provider address, whitelist-manager, and silo address must be read from chain before any classification of independence.
</Warning>

## Zodiac scope

The scope of the Zodiac Role Modifier granted to the Curator Safe determines which strategy actions Bizantine can execute autonomously. This must be inspected on chain per vault.
