## What it is

A Direct Bizantine vault is a Bizantine-owned on-chain stack:

- **SleeveVault** — shares vault (ERC-4626-derived)
- **NAVSourcedSleeve** — accepts NAV updates from a NAVSource
- **NAVSource** — the on-chain NAV feed submitted to by the NAV signer
- **Strategy adapters** — e.g. `GenericERC4626Adapter` for Morpho Mode B
- **Safe** owner (3-of-5) with timelock
- **Fordefi** curator, keeper, and NAV signer keys
- **Hypernative** guardian for auto-pause

## Why it exists

- **Fallback substrate.** Any position on any other platform can, in principle, be re-homed into a Direct SleeveVault (subject to platform cooperation for exit).
- **Independent operability.** Bizantine controls every role by construction.
- **First write path.** The v0.1 Level-3 write demonstration uses the Direct stack: passive Morpho supply via `GenericERC4626Adapter`.

## Direct is not "one vault"

A Direct vault is a template. Each deployment is a separate SleeveVault \+ NAVSourcedSleeve \+ NAVSource \+ adapter set, registered independently in the vault registry with its own capacity, allowlist, and adapter roster.
