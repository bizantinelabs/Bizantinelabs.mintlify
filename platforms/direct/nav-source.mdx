## NAVSource

`NAVSource` is the on-chain contract that receives `submitNAV(...)` calls from the NAV signer (Fordefi, dedicated key). It enforces:

- `maxDeltaBpsPerHour · Δt` bound per single step
- `maxAge` freshness
- Signer role check
- Bounded corridors per vault

Breaches revert. A step outside the corridor requires a `forceNAV` path (2-of-3 or Safe \+ timelock, per configuration), never automated.

## NAVSourcedSleeve

`NAVSourcedSleeve` is the settlement side. It reads NAV from `NAVSource`, applies asset conservation and no-over-issuance checks, and mints or burns shares against the settled NAV.

## Bizantine-independent recompute

Before any `submitNAV`, the monitoring service independently recomputes NAV from chain (adapter reads, oracle reads with freshness \+ deviation checks) and compares to the intended submission. Mismatch beyond tolerance halts the submission and pages the on-call. See [NAV updates](/operations/nav-updates).
