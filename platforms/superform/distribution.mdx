Superform is treated as an optional **distribution** surface, not a routine execution environment. Depositor flow through SuperVault shells can bring LP breadth without requiring Bizantine to move book operations onto Superform infrastructure.

## Design intent (Phase 1.5\+)

- SuperVault → sleeve allowlisting: a SuperVault whose strategy root permits allocation into a Bizantine Direct SleeveVault as the underlying strategy
- Validators price the SuperVault; Bizantine independently prices the sleeve
- Emergency-withdraw hook in the strategy root pulls assets back to the sleeve or to a Bizantine-controlled address

## Preconditions before enabling

- Confirm which on-chain role (if any) Superform holds on the Bizantine SuperVault — read from chain
- Confirm the generic 4626 hook covers arbitrary Bizantine sleeve addresses
- Confirm validator willingness / configuration to price the sleeve
- Confirm strategy-root update process and SLA
- Confirm timelock scope and fee / points impact

Until each precondition is confirmed on chain, the write path stays out. See [Continuity](/platforms/superform/continuity).
