Phase 1.5\+: additional Base, Monad, and other approved chains as vaults are provided.

## Bridge exposure

Any cross-chain movement is treated as a `BRIDGE_IN_FLIGHT` position with its own valuation, timestamp, and risk aging. Bridges are never treated as instant. Aging beyond configured thresholds alerts.

## Per-chain isolation

A chain-level outage (RPC unavailable, sequencer halted) does not degrade other chains' operation. Each chain has its own worker, its own RPC pool, and its own health check.
