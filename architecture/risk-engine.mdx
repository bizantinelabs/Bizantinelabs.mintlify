title: "Risk engine"
description: "Cross-platform exposure aggregation and platform-dependency risk."
---

The risk engine consumes `NormalizedPosition` records from every connector and aggregates exposure across the entire book. Wrappers never hide underlying exposure.

## Aggregation dimensions

Exposure is summed across:

- Protocol (Morpho, Euler, Symbiotic, ...)
- Chain
- Underlying asset
- Collateral asset
- Debt asset
- Oracle
- Market
- Vault
- Operator (Symbiotic)
- Network (Symbiotic)
- Counterparty
- Platform provider (Upshift, Lagoon, IPOR, Superform, Direct)

### Example

Morpho exposure reached via IPOR \+ via Direct \+ via a future Upshift or Lagoon vault aggregates to:

- One combined Morpho number
- One combined per-market number
- One combined oracle-dependency number

## Platform-dependency risk

A first-class category. See [Platform dependency risk](/risk/platform-dependency).

Any single non-Direct platform holding \> 25% of Bizantine NAV with `routineOperationsRequirePlatform || withdrawalsRequirePlatform || navRequiresPlatform == true` pages the on-call. Hard ceiling: **≤ 20% NAV per non-Direct platform** until independent unwind is demonstrated.

## On-chain hard controls

- Per-adapter, per-protocol, per-asset caps
- Target and selector allowlists
- Slippage and deadline
- NAV delta and staleness
- Minimum idle buffer
- Hardcoded target addresses per adapter
- Pause switches

## Off-chain monitored

- Cross-platform aggregates
- Health factors (Euler, borrow positions)
- Concentrated liquidity ranges
- Oracle deviation across sources
- Redemption-coverage ratio
- Bridge aging
- Platform service health
- Platform-dependency %

## Protocol-specific overlays

- **Symbiotic**: operator / network / slasher concentration, epoch-locked haircut on withdrawing positions
- **Euler**: health factor, LTV, leverage (borrow disabled by default)
- **Morpho**: LLTV, utilization, bad-debt detection, queue-cap validation
