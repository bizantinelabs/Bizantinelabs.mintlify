Every connector emits data conforming to the normalized model. The control plane and every engine consume only normalized types.

## NormalizedVaultState

Core fields:

- `platformId`, `vaultAddress`, `chainId`
- `asset` (base asset address \+ decimals)
- `totalAssets`, `totalSupply`, `pps`
- `idle`, `deployed`, `pending`, `claimable`, `receivables`, `liabilities`
- `paused`, `pauseReasons`
- `nav`: `{ value, timestamp, methodologyVersion, source }`
- `roles`: mapping of role name → on-chain holder
- `dependencies`: `DependencySnapshot`
- `capabilities`: `PlatformCapabilities` (see [Permission matrix](/reference/permission-matrix))

Every position carries:

- `gross`, `liability`, `net`
- `liquid`, `locked`, `pending`, `claimable`
- `valuationSource`, `valuationTimestamp`, `valuationConfidence`
- `withdrawalDelaySeconds`
- Where applicable: `leverageRatio`, `healthFactor`, `liquidationThreshold`, `protocolMetadata`

<Warning>
  Liabilities and risk dimensions are mandatory. A debt or pending-withdrawal position without its liability field is a rejected record.
</Warning>

## Audit log

Postgres append-only. Every proposed action, permission determination, simulation result, and submitted transaction is recorded. See [Keeper service](/developers/keeper-service).
