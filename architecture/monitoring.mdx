## Independent monitor

A separate process reads chain state, recomputes NAV independently of the keeper, and compares against the submitted NAV. Delta \> 5 bps fires an alert. Delta \> 75 bps auto-pauses via Hypernative.

## Metrics (Grafana)

- Price per share (per vault)
- NAV staleness
- Per-platform exposure and dependency %
- Permission `BLOCK` counts per capability
- Adapter utilization
- Redemption coverage ratio
- Bridge aging (where applicable)

## Hypernative auto-pause rules

Auto-pause triggers via the sentinel role:

- PPS delta beyond bound / NAV recompute mismatch \> 75 bps
- Unexpected outbound transfer or unauthorized approval
- Adapter-target exploit signal
- Cross-platform: same underlying protocol flagged anywhere → pause every Bizantine vault exposed to it

## Alerts (no auto-pause)

- Oracle cross-source deviation or staleness
- HF, CL range, buffer breach
- Keeper staleness
- Platform service health degraded
- Platform-dependency % over threshold
- Any capability transitions to `UNAVAILABLE` (role changed under us, platform paused, API down)

## Isolation guarantee

<Note>
  A failure in one connector must never stop monitoring or operation of other vaults. Connectors are isolated processes.
</Note>

## Runbooks

Every registered vault ships with a runbook covering: platform-API outage, keeper outage, operator outage, NAV-service outage, platform suspension, contractual termination, compromised operator, contract pause, upgrade dispute, strategy removal, emergency withdrawal, controlled migration, user communications.
