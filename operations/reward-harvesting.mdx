## Harvest

Adapters expose `harvest(data)` → `harvestedValue`. Rewards are pulled to a Bizantine-controlled sink (SleeveVault or a dedicated `RewardsManager`).

## Reward valuation

Rewards may be non-collateral tokens with their own oracle path. Every reward position carries:

- `valuation.source`
- `valuation.timestamp`
- `valuation.confidence`

Stale-reward valuation triggers an alert and excludes the reward from `netAssets` until refreshed.

## Permission

Harvest passes through the permission engine like any other adapter call. Fordefi policies restrict harvest destinations to registered sinks.

## IPOR RewardsManager

Harvests via IPOR's `RewardsManager` fuse. Reward types and destinations are subject to the same allowlist as any other fuse action.
