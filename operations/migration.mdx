---
title: "Migration"
description: "Re-home a position from a platform vault to a Direct SleeveVault."
---

Migration moves an underlying position from one platform's execution environment to another (typically to a Direct SleeveVault as the safe substrate).

## Preconditions

- Emergency-unwind path on source vault is verified (see [Continuity matrix](/reference/continuity-matrix))
- Destination SleeveVault \+ adapter registered
- Migration plan generated by `connector.getMigrationPlan(vault)`

## Steps

1. Pause source vault
2. `emergencyExit` on every adapter within the source vault (or platform-native unwind)
3. Reconcile net assets
4. Transfer assets to destination Direct SleeveVault (via Bizantine-controlled address)
5. Register position on destination adapter
6. Resume operations on destination

## Platform-specific notes

- **Direct**: no external cooperation required
- **IPOR**: no external cooperation once Atomist / Alpha are Bizantine-controlled
- **Upshift**: co-signed with partner multisig required
- **Lagoon**: possible if Bizantine holds curator; otherwise curator cooperation required
- **Superform**: wind-down subject to platform cooperation — no MSA in place
