## Direct

Curator withdraws from adapters as needed to restore idle balance; depositor `redeem` / `withdraw` served against the settled NAV.

## IPOR Fusion

Instant-withdraw fuse (where configured) or scheduled `WithdrawManager`: `request → Alpha releaseFunds → user redeemFromRequest`.

## Upshift

Withdrawal liquidity buffer serves immediate redemptions; larger redemptions require Vault Operator processing.

## Lagoon (ERC-7540)

`requestRedeem` → valuation-provider proposes NAV → curator settles → user `claim` after settlement.

## Symbiotic

**Never model as instant.** `requestWithdraw` → epoch delay 1–2 epochs (7–14 days) → claim. Pending amounts remain slashable until epoch ends.

## Withdrawal liability management

The control plane tracks:

- Outstanding share value vs claimable assets
- Pending withdrawal balances by platform / vault
- Redemption-coverage ratio
- Withdrawal-delay-weighted liquidity

Alert on redemption coverage below configured threshold. See [NAV risk](/risk/nav-risk) and [Liquidity](/risk/liquidity).
