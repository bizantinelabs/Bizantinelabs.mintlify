---
title: "Deposit processing"
description: "Deposit flow per platform."
---

## Direct

Allowlisted depositor calls `deposit(assets, receiver)` on the SleeveVault at the settled NAV. Curator-only in v0.1; third-party depositors require additional withdrawal and NAV-fairness review before enabling.

## IPOR Fusion

Standard ERC-4626 deposit into the Plasma Vault at NAV computed from Balance Fuses. Anti-sandwich deposit↔redeem timelock applies.

## Upshift

Standard ERC-4626 into the Upshift vault. NAV validated by multi-oracle \+ Max-%-Change ceiling.

## Lagoon (ERC-7540)

Async: `requestDeposit` → valuation-provider proposes NAV → curator settles → depositor `claim`. Deposits held in silo until settlement.

## Superform

Async via SuperVault triad, priced by validator quorum. Not an operational input for Bizantine in v0.1.
