## Purpose

`GenericERC4626Adapter` operates any ERC-4626 vault as an underlying strategy position. It:

- Deposits assets into a hardcoded target vault
- Values position via `convertToAssets(shares)`
- Withdraws via standard ERC-4626 `withdraw` / `redeem`
- Exposes `emergencyExit` that attempts full withdraw within available liquidity

## Constraints

- Target vault address is **hardcoded** at deployment; not configurable
- Selector targets restricted to `deposit`, `withdraw`, `redeem`, view methods
- No approval to any address other than the target vault
- No arbitrary `call` surface

## Not suitable for

- Async (ERC-7540) vaults — use ERC-7540 adapter
- Vaults with custom fee-on-transfer behavior on the underlying asset
- Vaults whose `convertToAssets` is manipulable (verify per target)

## Verification before use

- Read target vault's role holders, timelocks, and upgrade authority
- Confirm `convertToAssets` matches independent balance-based valuation
- Confirm `withdraw` behavior under high utilization
- Confirm no reentrancy surface exposed to depositors
