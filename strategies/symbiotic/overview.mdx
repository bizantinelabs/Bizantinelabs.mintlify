## Architecture

A Symbiotic vault is composed of:

- **Vault (accounting)**
- **Delegator** — NetworkRestake / FullRestake / OperatorSpecific / OperatorNetworkSpecific
- **Slasher** or **VetoSlasher**
- **Burner**
- **Resolver** (veto)

Key properties:

- **7-day epoch**
- Deposit **instant**
- Withdraw = **request → claim**, delay 1–2 epochs (7–14 days)
- Withdrawn funds remain slashable until epoch ends
- **Single collateral per vault**
- Rewards external (`defaultStakerRewards`); can be non-collateral tokens

## Do not model Symbiotic as continuously-liquid ERC-4626

Accounting must distinguish:

- Gross deposited
- Active delegated
- Withdrawable
- Pending-withdrawal
- Claimable
- Slashed-or-impaired
- Accrued-unclaimed rewards
- Epoch-locked

See [Withdrawals](/strategies/symbiotic/withdrawals) and [Slashing risk](/strategies/symbiotic/slashing-risk).
