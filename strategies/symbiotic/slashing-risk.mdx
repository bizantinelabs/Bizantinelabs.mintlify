## Slashing model

- `Slasher` — base slasher; instant slashing per protocol logic
- `VetoSlasher` — slashing proposals with a veto window enforced by a Resolver

Burner receives slashed collateral. Resolver can veto slashing proposals within the window (for VetoSlasher).

## Concentration limits

- Max operator concentration — no single operator \> X% of a Symbiotic position
- Max network concentration — no single network \> Y% of a Symbiotic position
- Max slasher concentration — for `VetoSlasher`, monitor Resolver identity
- Max Symbiotic-total concentration — across all Symbiotic positions in the book

## Locked-value haircut

Pending withdrawals are still slashable this epoch. The risk engine applies an **epoch-locked haircut** — a discount reflecting slashing risk during the pending period.

## Reward valuation

Rewards may be paid in non-collateral tokens with independent liquidity. Reward valuation is subject to `staleRewardValuation` policy: values older than a threshold are marked stale and excluded from NAV.

## Emergency exit

<Warning>
  Symbiotic emergency exit is bounded by the epoch mechanics. Positions cannot be exited faster than the withdrawal delay. This is a structural constraint, not a policy choice.
</Warning>

Emergency exit in Symbiotic:

1. Submit withdraw requests for the full active balance
2. Wait 1–2 epochs (7–14 days)
3. Claim after epoch ends

During the delay, positions remain slashable. Alerts fire and the risk engine flags Symbiotic exposure as `EMERGENCY_EXIT_IN_PROGRESS` for concentration accounting.
