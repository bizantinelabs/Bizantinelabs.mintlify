## On-chain hard controls

- Approved EVaults
- Approved collateral and debt allowlists
- Max LTV per position
- Min HF
- Max borrow
- Max leverage (default: 1x)
- Max utilization
- Min withdraw liquidity
- Approved EVC calls

## Off-chain monitoring

- Health factor per subaccount
- Cross-account HF for netting logic
- Oracle deviation across sources
- Utilization
- Available liquidity
- EVault pause state

## Emergency deleverage

<Warning>
  Not executed in v0.1. Documented design only.
</Warning>

Emergency deleverage path:

1. Guardian pauses further borrowing
2. Controlled unwind via EVC batching: repay debt using idle \+ collateral withdrawal
3. Bounded slippage on any DEX legs
4. Max-unwind-loss ceiling
5. Position closed to available liquidity
