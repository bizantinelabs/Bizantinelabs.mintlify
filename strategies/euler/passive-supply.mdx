## Position shape

Passive supply is an ERC-4626 deposit into an approved `EVault`. `NormalizedPosition.type = EULER_SUPPLY`. Value = `convertToAssets(shares)`.

## Controls

- Approved EVaults allowlist
- Max protocol allocation (aggregated across every wrapper)
- Max utilization
- Minimum withdraw liquidity
- Oracle deviation bound
- Max slippage / max unwind loss

## Fork tests

Supply → accrue → withdraw; liquidity shortage; oracle deviation; unauthorized EVault (must revert).

## Emergency

- `emergencyExit()` — attempts full withdraw within available liquidity
- Cross-platform propagation on adapter-target exploit signal
