<Warning>
  Collateralized borrow and leveraged loop are **disabled by default**. Enabling requires an explicit vault mandate, risk-committee approval, and a new adapter with hardcoded targets and selectors.
</Warning>

## Concepts

- **Controller / collateral enablement** — accounts must enable a controller before borrowing and enable collaterals for that controller (EVC)
- **Subaccounts** — up to 256 per address via address XOR; used to isolate risk of individual positions
- **Batching** — multiple actions atomically via EVC
- **Health factor** — must remain above `1`; below → liquidatable

## Position shapes (when enabled)

- `EULER_COLLATERAL` — collateral pledged
- `EULER_DEBT` — outstanding borrow, tracked as a `liability`
- `EULER_LEVERAGED_LOOP` — recursive supply/borrow with net exposure \> 1x

## Controls

- Approved collateral / debt / EVC calls allowlists
- Max LTV, min HF, max borrow, max leverage
- Oracle deviation, max slippage, max unwind loss
- Emergency deleverage path (designed, not enabled v0.1)

## Fork tests

Supply collateral → borrow → repay → withdraw; loop → partial unwind → full unwind; HF breach; emergency deleverage; unauthorized collateral / debt (must revert).
