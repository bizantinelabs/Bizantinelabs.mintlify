---
title: "What is Bizantine Vault OS?"
description: "Overview of the multi-platform vault control plane."
---

Bizantine Vault OS is a control plane that operates vaults across independent execution environments — Upshift, Lagoon, IPOR Fusion, Superform, and Direct Bizantine vaults — through one normalized model.

## What it does

- **Reads and reconciles** state, NAV, and positions across every platform Bizantine touches
- **Normalizes** vaults and positions into one common schema
- **Determines permissions** for any action — role holder, threshold, Fordefi policy, platform allowlist, timelock, pause, NAV freshness, simulability, independent unwind
- **Aggregates risk** across protocols, chains, assets, and platforms
- **Constructs and simulates** transactions per platform's native contracts, never bypassing platform access controls
- **Executes** only when every permission check resolves positively — `Unknown = Blocked`

## What it does not do

- It does not custody assets. Platforms and the underlying protocols hold custody.
- It does not replace platform access controls. Every write must pass both the platform's native access control **and** a Fordefi policy encoding the same envelope.
- It does not treat any platform as authoritative for Bizantine's book. NAV, positions, and history are independently reconstructed from chain wherever technically possible.
