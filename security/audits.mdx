<Warning>
  Vault OS v0.1 has not completed a full external audit. Capital caps apply until external review is complete.
</Warning>

## Pre-audit ceilings

- Direct sleeve v0.1: $$50,000 initially, scaling to $$150,000 total pre-audit
- No scaling past \$150,000 without external review
- No third-party depositors until additional withdrawal \+ NAV-fairness review

## External review scope (upcoming)

- Sleeve / NAVSource / adapter contracts
- Permission engine correctness
- Invariant suite completeness
- Fordefi policy envelope adequacy
- Emergency pause and unwind paths

## Post-audit expansion

- Cap increases per [Phase 1.5 roadmap](/ship-plan/roadmap)
- Third-party deposits gated on additional review
- Full audit before unrestricted caps
