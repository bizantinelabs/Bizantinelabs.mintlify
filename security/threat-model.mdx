---
title: "Threat model"
description: "What Vault OS defends against and where the boundary is."
---

## In scope

- Adapter target exploit (protocol drain, oracle manipulation) — auto-pause \+ cross-platform propagation
- Unauthorized signer action — dual-lock (native \+ Fordefi) prevents cross-envelope actions
- Compromise of a single key — bounded envelopes and simulation limit blast radius
- NAV manipulation — independent recompute \+ bounded steps \+ Hypernative pause on mismatch
- Async double-spend attempts — request/claim state machine invariants
- Platform service outage — read/monitor continues; write halts until independent path re-verified
- Silent role reassignment — permission engine re-reads on every action; alert on capability transitions

## Out of scope

- Underlying protocol governance decisions (e.g. Morpho market removal by curator) — monitored; reaction is manual
- Platform-level upgrade authority abuse where Bizantine has no on-chain role
- Off-chain platform service takeover (Superform bundler, August policy engine) beyond emergency exit paths that require platform cooperation

## Boundary

Bizantine's protection ends where the platform's contractual and technical authority begins. Where those authorities cannot be verified on chain or cannot be exercised independently, the platform-dependency ceiling caps exposure.
