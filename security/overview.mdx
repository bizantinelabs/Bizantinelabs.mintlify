## Trust model

- **Contracts**: Direct contracts follow the v0 audited pattern. IPOR Plasma Vault core is immutable; fuses are non-upgradable. Platform contracts (Upshift, Lagoon, Superform) are subject to their own upgrade authority.
- **Keys**: Safe 3-of-5 \+ timelock, Fordefi curator / keeper / NAV signer, Hypernative guardian sentinel.
- **Roles on external platforms**: only Bizantine-controlled where on-chain-verified.
