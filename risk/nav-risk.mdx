## Sources of NAV error

- Stale oracle price
- Oracle deviation across sources
- Rebasing behavior not captured by adapter
- Bad-debt propagation from underlying markets
- Reward valuation drift (stale non-collateral tokens)
- Slashing events (Symbiotic) between requests and epoch end
- Bridge in-flight positions during transit

## Mitigations

- Multi-source price validation
- Independent NAV recompute in the monitor process
- Bounded delta per submission (`maxDeltaBpsPerHour · Δt`)
- Force-NAV path (2-of-3 or Safe) for legitimate large moves, always audit-logged
- Stale-reward exclusion
- Epoch-locked haircut for Symbiotic pending positions
- `BRIDGE_IN_FLIGHT` aging

## Alert rules

- Independent recompute delta \> 5 bps — alert
- Delta \> 75 bps — auto-pause via Hypernative
- Oracle deviation across sources \> threshold — alert
- NAV staleness \> `maxStaleness` — block new deposits / actions consuming NAV

## Corrected invariants

The original v0 invariant "PPS never decreases" was removed. A loss-bearing vault can legitimately lose value. The corrected invariants are documented in [Invariants](/security/invariants):

- No over-issuance
- Asset conservation
- Accounting identity (mint vs burn)
- PPS moves only for cause
- No unauthorized extraction
- Allowlist holds
- Withdrawal correctness (async)
- Pause safety
