---
title: "Liquidity risk"
description: "Liquid, pending, and claimable accounting."
---

Every position separates:

- `liquidAssets` — immediately withdrawable
- `pendingAssets` — in a request / claim queue or epoch
- `liabilities` — debt or outstanding obligations
- `claimable` — subset of pending currently claimable

## Rules

- Never model an async or epoch-locked position as continuously liquid
- Symbiotic pending withdrawals are illiquid until the epoch ends, even though a request has been made
- Instant-withdraw fuses (IPOR) count as liquid only up to the actual on-chain available liquidity in the underlying market

## Redemption-coverage ratio

```text
RCR = (liquidAssets + claimable) / outstanding_redemption_liability
```

Alert on RCR below configured threshold. Halt new redemption acceptance below hard floor.

## Withdrawal-delay-weighted liquidity

For forecasting, positions are weighted by `withdrawalDelaySeconds`:

```text
L_eff = sum_i liquidAssets_i + sum_j pendingAssets_j * exp(-lambda * delay_j)
```

The control plane uses `L_eff` to size acceptable redemption commitments.
