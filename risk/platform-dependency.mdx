---
title: "Platform dependency risk"
description: "First-class risk category introduced in v0.1."
---

Platform dependency risk quantifies how much Bizantine's book depends on any one non-Direct platform's off-chain services.

## Signals

Per platform, the connector's `DependencySnapshot` reports:

- `routineOperationsRequirePlatform`
- `withdrawalsRequirePlatform`
- `navRequiresPlatform`
- `emergencyExitRequiresPlatform`
- `migrationRequiresPlatform`

## Alert threshold

> If any single platform holds \> **25%** of Bizantine NAV **and** (`routineOperationsRequirePlatform` ∪ `withdrawalsRequirePlatform` ∪ `navRequiresPlatform`), page the on-call.

## Hard ceiling

> ≤ **20%** NAV per non-Direct platform until that platform's independent-unwind is demonstrated on chain.
