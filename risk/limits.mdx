title: "Risk limits"
description: "v0.1 hard limits."
---

## Per-tx and per-day

- Curator per-tx: ≤ \$50,000
- Curator per-day: ≤ \$150,000
- IPOR Alpha per-action (once role confirmed): small-notional cap (low four-figures) per action

## Vault caps

- Direct sleeve v0.1: $$50,000 initially, scaling to $$150,000 total pre-audit
- No scaling past \$150,000 without external review

## Platform concentration

- Non-Direct platform: **≤ 20% NAV** until independent unwind demonstrated
- Alert at 25% for any platform with routine-ops / withdrawal / NAV dependency

## Protocol concentration

- Configurable per protocol; caps set by risk committee
- Wrapper-blind aggregation — exposure counts across all platforms

## NAV movement

- `maxDeltaBpsPerHour` per vault
- Force-NAV path for legitimate large moves (Safe or 2-of-3)
- Auto-pause on \> 75 bps unexplained delta

## Withdrawal buffer

- Min idle % per vault (configurable, on chain)
- Redemption-coverage alert threshold

## Leverage

- **Default: prohibited.** Borrow, leverage, and collateralized flows disabled by default.
- Enable only under explicit vault mandate with risk-committee approval.

## Incident severity levels

| Level | Trigger | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV-1 | Funds at direct risk, exploit signal, PPS delta \> 75 bps | Auto-pause; on-call paged; incident room opened |
| SEV-2 | Platform-dependency threshold breach, recompute \> 5 bps | Alert; on-call paged; investigate |
| SEV-3 | Oracle stale, keeper stale, service degradation | Alert; SRE investigates |
| SEV-4 | Informational | Log |
