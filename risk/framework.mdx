Risk is aggregated across every platform by:

- Protocol
- Chain
- Underlying asset
- Collateral asset
- Debt asset
- Oracle
- Market
- Vault
- Operator
- Network
- Counterparty
- Platform provider

A wrapper never hides underlying exposure. See [Risk engine](/architecture/risk-engine).

## Hard on-chain controls

Per-adapter / protocol / asset caps, allowlists, slippage / deadline, NAV delta \+ staleness, min idle buffer, hardcoded selectors, pause.

## Off-chain monitored

Cross-platform aggregates, HF, CL ranges, oracle deviation, redemption-coverage ratio, bridge aging, platform service health, platform-dependency %.

## Incident severity levels

- **SEV-1** — funds at risk. Auto-pause on triggers; page immediately.
- **SEV-2** — operational disruption. Alert; investigate.
- **SEV-3** — informational anomaly. Log; review at next window.
