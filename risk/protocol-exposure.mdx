title: "Protocol exposure"
description: "Aggregated across every wrapper."
---

Protocol exposure is aggregated across every platform. Morpho reached via IPOR \+ via Direct \+ via Upshift \+ via Lagoon = one combined Morpho number, one combined per-market number, one combined oracle-dependency number.

Enforced by making the risk engine consume `NormalizedPosition.protocol` regardless of which connector produced it.

## Dimensions tracked

- Per protocol
- Per (protocol, chain)
- Per (protocol, market or vault ID)
- Per (protocol, oracle)
- Per (protocol, collateral asset) / (protocol, debt asset)

## Caps

Caps are set at the aggregated level, not per-wrapper. Allocating additional capital to Morpho via IPOR when the aggregated Morpho cap is already at limit → rejected before build.

## Cross-platform propagation

If the same underlying protocol is flagged as compromised anywhere in the world, Hypernative pauses **every** Bizantine vault exposed to that protocol — not only the connector that raised the flag.
