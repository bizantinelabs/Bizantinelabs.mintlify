## Design premise

Bizantine capital sits inside multiple vault frameworks — IPOR, Euler, Morpho, and potentially Upshift and Lagoon — each with different role models, NAV mechanics, and withdrawal semantics. Without a control plane above them, each platform becomes a de-facto operating system for its slice of the book. Vault OS is Bizantine's owned read / normalize / permission / risk / act layer that treats every platform as an interchangeable execution environment.
